# Contributing to Montage

Montage is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or (at your option) any later version
([LICENSE](LICENSE)), with the additional permission in [LICENSE-EXCEPTION](LICENSE-EXCEPTION). Contributions are
accepted under the same terms.

## Sign your commits

Each commit must carry a `Signed-off-by:` line (`git commit -s`), which certifies the
[Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/): that you wrote the change, or otherwise have
the right to submit it under Montage's licence.

## Code from elsewhere

- Do not paste code, data or model weights from another project unless its licence allows it under GPL-3.0-or-later
  (MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, LGPL and GPL-3.0-compatible licences do; "non-commercial", "research only" and GPL-2.0-only
  terms do not).
- Keep the original copyright notice in the file, say what it was ported from, and add the component to
  [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) (with its licence text in `LICENSES/` when it is not already there).
- Do not copy text, icons or artwork from other products or their manuals.

## Names

Name features by what they do. Do not use another product's feature name, product name or logo as the name of a
Montage feature. Naming another product to say Montage works with it (a file format, a control surface, a plug-in
standard) is fine.
