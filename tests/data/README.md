# Test data

- `jfk.wav`: 11 seconds of President Kennedy's 1961 inaugural address ("ask not what your country can do for you"), 16 kHz mono. A US government work in the public domain; the same clip whisper.cpp ships in its `samples/` folder. Used by the transcription tests.
- `faces/`: three public-domain portraits, shrunk to 320 pixels wide, for the people-search tests (two of John F. Kennedy, one of Neil Armstrong):
  - `jfk-color.jpg`: [John F. Kennedy, White House color photo portrait](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_F._Kennedy,_White_House_color_photo_portrait.jpg), Cecil W. Stoughton, 1963 (US National Archives, NAID 194255). A work of the US federal government, in the public domain.
  - `jfk-looking-up.jpg`: [JFK White House portrait, looking up](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:JFK_White_House_portrait_looking_up_lighting_corrected.jpg), White House Press Office. A work of the US federal government, in the public domain.
  - `armstrong.jpg`: [Neil Armstrong](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Neil_Armstrong_pose.jpg), NASA photo S69-31741, 1969. A work of NASA, in the public domain.
- `c2pa/`: a throwaway certificate authority (`ca.pem`) and an ES256 signing certificate it issued (`chain.pem`, with `signer.key`), made with OpenSSL for the Content Credentials tests. They sign nothing real, and nothing trusts them unless a test adds `ca.pem` as a trust anchor; the CA's own key was not kept.
- `lensfun/test-lenses.xml`: a small lens database in lensfun's format (version 1) for the lens correction tests: three cameras, a mount that takes another by adapter, and three lenses. The Samyang 8mm fisheye and GoPro HERO11 Black entries are lensfun's own (lensfun.github.io, CC BY-SA 3.0), cut down; the Sony zoom is made up from a real lens's measurements. The tests compare Montage's corrections with numbers lensfun 0.3.4 computed from this file.
- `clf/`: Common LUT Format files made up for the CLF reader's tests (written by `clf/make_fixtures.py`): every operator Montage reads, integer bit depths, and both 3D LUT interpolations. The tests compare Montage's results with OpenColorIO's for the same files.
- `sofa/`: a made-up head in SOFA (AES69 SimpleFreeFieldHRIR, 44.1 kHz, 26 directions, 32 taps), whose ear nearer a sound hears it earlier and louder, as `small.sofa` and zipped as `small.zip` (written by `sofa/make_fixture.py` with the netCDF4 module), for the binaural ambisonics tests.
