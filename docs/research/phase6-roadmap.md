# Phase 6 roadmap (October 2026)

A fifth gap analysis, made as Phase 5 drew to a close. It compares Montage with Premiere 26.0 to 26.5, DaVinci Resolve 21, 21.1 and 21.1.1, Final Cut Pro 12.0 to 12.4 and Media Composer 2026.8 (with Avid and FilmLight's September 2026 plan to put Baselight colour inside it). Every candidate was checked against `README.md` and the `PROGRESS.md` checklists and their "Not done" notes. Paper Edit, OGraf, MCP agents, multi-object masks, lens correction, reference output, the grade graph, Python scripting, render nodes, 3D camera tracking, compare timelines, gap editing, Clear Solo, clip durations, ProRes RAW settings and the 21.1 camera logs are all already there. Dolby Vision, per-bin locking and direct publishing were declined earlier and stay out.

Ranking is value to a professional editor or colourist × feasibility in C++/Qt/FFmpeg with optional local ONNX models. Nothing below needs a paid SDK, an account or an API key. Licences are permissive unless flagged. Where a free vendor runtime is the only route (Blackmagic RAW, Dolby's speech gate), it is loaded or fetched as DeckLink and NDI already are: the runtime is never shipped, and Montage works without it. Sizes: S ≤ 2, M 2–6, L 6–12 person-weeks.

## Earlier leftovers carried forward

- **GPU compositor** (Phase 2 #15), **Motion Deblur** (Phase 4 #12), **Speed Editor** (#13c), **Tangent** (#13d) and **MV-HEVC** (#15) are still open. The research for the last four is in `phase5-roadmap.md` and still holds; Resolve 21 now ships AI Motion Deblur and an MV-HEVC render, which raises those two.
- **Prompted sound isolation** (Phase 4 #6b) still has no model with usable terms. **Temporally consistent inpainting** is in the same position.
- The **reference output** notes listed capture as not done. Capture is now #13.

## Phase 6 candidates

| # | Feature | Who has it | Size | Notes |
|---|---------|-----------|------|-------|
| 1 | GPU compositor and grade: decode upload, transform, blend modes, the colour effects, LUTs, colour-space and OCIO transforms and the grade graph on the GPU, real time at 4K | All four (Resolve is GPU-first) | L | QRhi (Qt, LGPL-3.0), which `app/HdrSurface` already uses through `<rhi/qrhi.h>` (Qt 6.6+; Ubuntu 24.04's Qt 6.4 keeps the CPU path). Use compute and fragment shaders in Vulkan-style GLSL, baked offline by `qsb` as `scripts/bake-hdr-shaders.sh` already does. `qsb` is GPL-3.0 with Qt's tool exception, but it runs only at bake time and the baked output is committed. Order of work: YUV planes (8–16-bit) to linear RGB in a shader; crop, transform and the 13 blend modes; Color Correct; Curves and Hue Curves as 1D textures; `.cube` LUTs as 3D textures; `render/ColorSpace` curves and gamuts; OCIO through its `GpuShaderDesc` (it emits GLSL); HDR Palette; `core/GradeGraph`'s mixers and keys. Any effect without a kernel reads the frame back to the CPU path in `render/Processing` and uploads it again, so nothing is lost. Frames stay on the GPU into the HDR viewer and reference output, and exports and the render cache use the same path. Test GPU against CPU renders within a tolerance on Mesa's lavapipe (MIT) in CI and WARP on Windows. Every remaining effect would make it XL; keep those for later |
| 2 | Blackmagic RAW (.braw) decoding with RAW settings: ISO, white balance, tint, exposure, highlight recovery, gamut and gamma, sidecars, and half and quarter decode for playback | Resolve; Premiere, Media Composer and Final Cut through Blackmagic's free plugins | M | Blackmagic RAW SDK 6.0. Blackmagic says it is free and public, but its runtime's redistribution terms are unconfirmed, so **flag** it. Load `BlackmagicRawAPI` (framework, DLL or `.so`) at run time from the user's installed Blackmagic RAW or SDK, as `audio/DynLib` loads Desktop Video. Declare its COM interfaces by hand, as braw-rs does, unless the SDK headers prove to carry the permissive notice DeckLink's do. Decode in `media/Decoder` as a new source kind beside `media/ProResRaw`, into Blackmagic Wide Gamut Gen 5 / Film Gen 5, which `render/ColorSpace` already has. Put the settings in Interpret Footage's RAW settings, and read and write `.sidecar` files. Without the runtime, .braw comes in offline with a message |
| 3 | Tangent colour panels (Elements, Ripple, Wave 2, Arc) | Resolve, Premiere (Lumetri), Baselight | M | TUBE 3.2 / TIPC rev 4 over TCP to Tangent Hub on 127.0.0.1:64246. Messages are big-endian and length-prefixed: the app sends ApplicationDefinition and ParameterValue and receives ParameterChange and MenuChange. Controls and default maps are XML files. Use Hammerspoon's `hs.tangent` and CommandPost's manager (both MIT) as references. Put a `control/Tangent` beside `control/Hui` and `control/Osc`. Trackballs map to the lift, gamma, gain and offset wheels and the HDR Palette zones; knobs to Color Correct, Curves and Colour Warper; buttons to node navigation (`core/GradeGraph`), grade versions, grab still and the transport. Moves merge into one undo step per touch. Needs Tangent Hub (Mac and Windows). The Developer Support Pack's terms are still unconfirmed, so **check** them |
| 4 | Colour-page navigation: a clip thumbnail strip, a lightbox, and a stills gallery (grab a still with its grade, wipe or split-screen against any still, apply a still's grade, albums) | Resolve (Gallery, Lightbox), Premiere 26.2 Colour Mode (Clip Grid), Baselight | S–M | In-house. Save stills as half-float EXR (the existing EXR writer) beside the project, each with its grade (graph, versions, group) as JSON. Use those files for the gallery panel and MCP `montage_gallery`. The wipe and split-screen reuse Compare with Reference's divider in the Program monitor and the Layouts cell maths, for viewing only. Draw the lightbox and strip from the render cache and thumbnails. Apply a grade through the same code as Paste Attributes with Colour nodes |
| 5 | HDR and SDR trim passes: one grade mastered in HDR, with per-target trims (SDR Rec.709 100 nits, HDR10 at 1000, 600 or 300 nits, HLG) by clip or scene, each previewed and rendered as its own deliverable | Resolve 21 MultiMaster Trim Manager, 21.1 individual trims (Studio) | M | In-house, open metadata only (not Dolby Vision). A sequence holds a list of targets. Each target is the existing tone mapping (HDR to SDR, PQ peak) followed by a trim layer per clip: lift, gamma, gain, saturation, a highlight roll-off and Mid-tone Detail from #10, keyframable. Trims are stored as a `GradeGraph` stage after the clip's grade. The viewer and reference output choose the target they show. Export Versions queues a render per target, and the HDR10+ analysis runs on HDR targets. Copy trims across clips with Paste Attributes |
| 6 | Dialogue-gated loudness: measure the "Dialog LKFS" Netflix asks for (−27 ±2 LKFS, −2 dBTP, the programme figure when dialogue is under 15 %), as a meter, a Quality Check target and an export normalisation | Fairlight, Pro Tools (with Nugen VisLM), iZotope Insight | S | BS.1770 gated on speech in `media/Loudness`. The gate comes from `render/AutoMix`'s speech detection and the Dialogue role and transcripts, run on the full mix. Report the percentage that is dialogue. Dolby's speech-gating reference code (Dialogue Intelligence) is the measure Netflix QC uses. It is a free download, but under a revocable EULA that allows only object code in professional meters (**flag**: not open source). Fetch it at build time when present and label Montage's own gate as an estimate otherwise. Add a Netflix preset to the live meter, Quality Check and Export › Loudness |
| 7 | CEA-708 captions: native DTVCC services next to the 608 already embedded, 708 read back, MCC sidecars and 708 in broadcast MXF | Premiere (CEA-708 tracks), Resolve 21.1 (several embedded caption tracks in MXF and QuickTime), Media Composer with MacCaption | S–M | In-house writer for CTA-708 service blocks: DefineWindow, SetPenAttributes and SetPenColor, the text, then Display and Hide Window, for pop-on, roll-up and paint-on, from `core/Captions` styles and placement. Interleave them as cc_type 2/3 triplets with the 608 pairs that the SCC writer in `core/Captions` already puts in A/53 cc_data. Read them back from the decoder's A/53 side data. libcaption (MIT) only wraps 608 in 708, so it serves as a cross-check, not the writer. Write MCC through FFmpeg 8.0's MCC muxer (an in-house text writer on older FFmpeg). Add a ST 436 ANC track to the broadcast MXF presets where FFmpeg carries it |
| 8 | Blackmagic Speed Editor and Editor Keyboard over USB and Bluetooth: jog, shuttle and scroll, source and timeline keys, trim and multicam keys, LEDs | Resolve | S–M | hidapi (BSD-3). The protocol is from smunaut/blackmagic-misc (Apache-2.0), with CommandPost's `hs.blackmagic` (MIT) as a reference: VID 0x1edb, PID 0xda0e; a challenge and response on feature report 6, renewed within about ten minutes; jog on report 3 (relative, absolute, absolute with dead zero); keys on report 4; LEDs on reports 2 and 4; battery on report 7. Add `control/SpeedEditor` mapping keys to existing commands: Trim mode, dynamic JKL, source edits, multicam angles 1–9, Close Up, Smart Insert. Resolve holds the device while it runs |
| 9 | Gamut tools: a CIE 1931/1976 chromaticity scope with Rec.709, P3 and Rec.2020 triangles, an out-of-gamut overlay, and ACES Reference Gamut Compression as an effect | Resolve (CIE scope, ACES RGC), Baselight | S | In-house. Add a new mode to `app/ScopesWidget`, plotting each sampled pixel's xy from the sequence's working space (HDR included) with gamut outlines. The overlay is a monitor view like the exposure checks. RGC uses the published formula and parameters (limits C 1.147, M 1.264, Y 1.312; thresholds 0.815, 0.803, 0.880; power 1.2) in AP1 after the input transform. Check it against the CTL to the spec's 0.002 conformance and against OCIO's `ACES_GamutComp13` where built with OCIO. It goes into exported LUTs |
| 10 | Film density and texture: ColorSlice-style hue vectors (six plus skin) with subtractive saturation and density; Mid-tone Detail and Texture Pop | Resolve 19+ (ColorSlice, Contrast Pop, Midtone Detail), Baselight (coming to Media Composer) | S–M | In-house. Density darkens each vector's colours as their saturation rises, in a log-like working encoding, with a soft hue weighting per vector, a centre, highlight protection and a global depth. It is colour-only, so it goes into exported LUTs. Detail is a band-pass (difference of Gaussians, or the colour-guided filter Refine Edges already uses) weighted to mid-tones and kept to luma so colour does not fringe. It scales with preview size as other sizes do. Both take masks, grade versions and node placement like any colour effect |
| 11 | Motion Deblur | Resolve 21 (AI Motion Deblur) | M | NAFNet-GoPro-width32 (code MIT; GoPro and REDS data CC BY 4.0, credit them). Export fp32 ONNX from the official `.pth` and host it pinned by revision and SHA-256. Run it in overlapping tiles on ONNX Runtime through `media/Inference`, and send heavy use to Render In to Out or Render and Replace, as `phase5-roadmap.md` sets out. Restormer (MIT) is the fallback |
| 12 | ACES Metadata Files (AMF) with CLF looks: import from on-set tools (input transform, CDLs, look LUTs, output transform per clip) and export with VFX pulls and dailies | Resolve 19.1+, Baselight 6+, Pomfort LiveGrade and Silverstack; Netflix asks for AMF on ACES shows | S–M | AMF is XML (Academy S-2019-001, namespace `urn:ampas:aces:amf:v2.0`; schema and examples Apache-2.0 at aces-aswf/aces-amf), read with QtXml. Map it onto what exists: the input transform to a camera log space or ACES IDT; `core/Cdl` for CDL looks; CLF look LUTs through OCIO's CLF reader when built with OCIO, else a small CLF reader for Matrix, Range, LUT1D, LUT3D, ASC_CDL, Log and Exponent; the output transform to OCIO's ACES 2.0. Clips match by `clipId`, file name or the `AMF_NAME`/`AMF_UUID` columns in `core/Ale`. Write one AMF per pulled shot beside the EXR or DPX files |
| 13 | Capture from SDI, HDMI and NDI: ingest a feed or a deck to ProRes, DNxHR or XDCAM with timecode and up to 16 channels, editable while it records; crash record and timed stops | Resolve (Media page capture), Media Composer | M | Use the same vendored headers and runtime loading as Reference Output. DeckLink input is `IDeckLinkInput` with RP 188 timecode (Linux and macOS first, as for output). NDI receive needs the free NDI Runtime, which is proprietary (only its headers are MIT). Encode through FFmpeg into fragmented MOV or MXF so `EditorState`'s growing-file support treats each recording as growing media and the clip can be cut at once. For tests, receive Montage's own NDI output. The DeckLink path stays unchecked without a card, as output is now |
| 14 | Higher-order ambisonics (to third order, 16 channels) with binaural monitoring through SOFA HRTFs | Resolve 19+ Fairlight (first to fifth order, binaural, head tracking), Pro Tools | M | Extend `core/Ambisonics` (first-order AmbiX, ACN/SN3D) with real spherical harmonics up to order 3, rotation by the Ivanic–Ruedenberg recursion so Reframe 360° and Pan to Follow still turn the field, and an AllRAD decoder to the BS.2051 layouts `core/Surround` has. For binaural, convolve virtual speakers with HRIRs read by libmysofa (BSD-3). The default is the SADIE II KU100 set (Apache-2.0, credit it), downloaded the first time, and any personal `.sofa` file can be chosen. Export HOA in the SA3D box with its order |
| 15 | Spatial video (MV-HEVC) in, and out where the hardware allows | Final Cut, Resolve 21 (MV-HEVC render) | M | As researched in `phase5-roadmap.md`. Decoding is a build-time feature on FFmpeg 7.1+ (`view_ids`, `AV_FRAME_DATA_VIEW_ID`), feeding the existing stereo pipeline's two eyes. Encoding uses `hevc_nvenc` (FFmpeg 8.0+) where the GPU reports the capability, with `movenc`'s spatial boxes. FFmpeg's x265 wrapper cannot encode MV-HEVC, and x265 is GPL in any case |

Lower priority: face reshaping (Resolve 21; a mesh warp from MediaPipe's Apache-2.0 face landmarker is feasible, ageing needs generative models); click-to-focus with bokeh shapes and tracked focus pulls (Resolve 21 CineFocus, Final Cut 12.4's Cinematic editing; Lens Blur (Depth) already keys focus); sky replacement (Resolve 21.1); DNG opcode lists for RAW stills (Resolve 21.1); splitting one export across render nodes; multi-layer EXR with Cryptomatte mattes (OpenEXR, BSD-3); HTJ2K in EXR and IMF (OpenJPH, BSD-2); asynchronous script calls (Resolve 21.1.1); linear-phase and dynamic EQ (hosted plugins already cover them).

Left out: Premiere 26.5's Generative Media Tool and cloud Generative Extend (cloud models and credits), Blackmagic Cloud presentations and Avid Content Core (accounts), direct social upload (credentials), MainConcept renders (paid), tethered stills capture on Resolve 21's Photo page (camera makers' SDKs), Dolby Vision (licensed).
