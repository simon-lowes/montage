# Phase 11 roadmap (October 2026)

A tenth gap analysis, made as Phase 10 drew to a close. It compares Montage with Premiere 26.5.2 (Premiere 27.0 has not been released; a guide dated 21 October says Adobe has given no date) and After Effects 26.5 with its beta (an AI Assistant, keyframeable 3D materials on text and shapes, and OCIO's built-in ACES 2.0 configs; After Effects 26.0 brought variable font animation), DaVinci Resolve 21.1 (8 September) and 21.1.1 (2 October; no 21.2 has been announced), Final Cut Pro 12.4 (29 September; the Final Cut Pro 13 shown in a video that leaked on 1 October is still unannounced) and Media Composer 2026.8 (shown at IBC; no 2026.10 was found). Adobe MAX is on 10–12 November, after this analysis. It also looks at Baselight 8 as previewed at IBC 2026 (due in 2027), Nuke 17.1 (no 17.2 was found; Foundry says 17.1 is the last release for Intel Macs), Pro Tools 2026.4 (28 April; no later release was found), Nuendo 15 (as Production Expert described it on 4 September), Kdenlive 26.08.2 (8 October) and Shotcut 26.9.27. Descript and CapCut were not looked at again. Among open tools it looks at FFmpeg 9.0.2 (18 September; 9.1 is being prepared, with a Vulkan APV encoder, a new AAC encoder and an LCEVC bitstream filter), OpenColorIO 2.6.0 (1 October, new since the Phase 10 analysis: SMPTE ST 2136-1 CLF, ASWF colour interop IDs, HDR display validation, new built-in ACES configs and Apple Log 2), OpenTimelineIO 0.18.1 (nothing newer), OpenFX 1.5.1 (its documentation is dated 9 October; no 1.6) and AV2 (the specification and the AVM 1.0 reference software, 28 May, announced on 9 June). It also covers SMPTE ST 2098-2 (IAB), ST 2067-50, -201 and -203, ITU-R BT.2446-1 and ISO 21727. Every candidate was checked against `README.md`, the source in `src/` and the `PROGRESS.md` checklists with their "Not done" and "Not yet" notes. These are already there and are not proposed again. From Phase 10: scene-referred grading, ST 2094-50 metadata, LUTs rebuilt as grades, grain matching, take lanes, the monitoring section, delivery paperwork, image subtitles, deep compositing, 3D models, ARA hosting, drafted SDH captions, bandwidth extension, screen reader access and trained effects. From Phase 9: De-Clip, De-Plosive, De-Wind and Breath Control, floating-point pictures, damaged-frame repair, dialogue intelligibility, sample-accurate sound, the phase and spectrum meters, upmixing, Avid bins, film counts and lists, the sound library, particles, APV and Open Media Transport. From earlier phases: everything the Phase 10 roadmap listed as already there. Recent releases also brought things Montage already has, so these are not proposed either: cutting, copying and pasting gaps, Inspector presets and several caption streams in QuickTime and MXF (Resolve 21.1); reading slates, blemish removal, face reshaping, refocusing by depth, search by objects, words and faces, speech in a trained voice, motion deblur, folder tracks, several HDR and SDR trims from one timeline, animation driven by sound, Lottie and OGraf (Resolve 21); ST 2136-1 CLF and Apple Log 2 (OpenColorIO 2.6; Montage reads and writes CLF and has Apple Log 2 itself); the Analyzer Track and ARA (Nuendo 15); searching footage for sounds (Premiere 26.5); OpenTimelineIO interchange and automatic ingest and sync (Media Composer 2026.8); round trips to an image editor (the leaked Final Cut Pro 13; Montage has Edit Original and reloads changed media). Dolby Vision, per-bin locking, direct publishing and generated media stay out.

Ranking is value to a professional editor, colourist or mixer × feasibility in C++/Qt/FFmpeg with optional local ONNX and GGUF models. Items 1–12 can be tested completely in CI and come first; 13–15 each have a part that cannot be checked here, said in the row. Nothing below needs a paid SDK, an account, a cloud API or an API key, and nothing proposed needs a new model. Every dependency is GPL-3.0-compatible unless **flagged**. The licences were read from each project's own LICENSE file on 26 October: tinyusdz (Apache-2.0), Niantic's `spz` (MIT), DTS's IABLib in `iab-renderer` (BSD-3-Clause), DTS's `iab-validator` (MIT, Xperi), `leqm-nrt` (GPL-3.0), AVM (BSD-3-Clause-Clear), VVenC (BSD-3-Clause-Clear) and SVT-JPEG-XS (a variant of the two-clause BSD licence). The original 3D Gaussian splatting code from Inria is for non-commercial research only, so #11 uses none of it. Everything can be built and tested in the Linux cloud container (and on macOS and Windows in the Package workflow) except where a row says otherwise. Sizes: S ≤ 2, M 2–6, L 6–12 person-weeks.

## Earlier leftovers carried forward

- **Phase 9 #5 and #11 stay out**, as the Phase 10 roadmap left them: the cleanest speaker separation model's weights name two licences, one of them not GPL-3.0-compatible (**flag**), and no vision-language model with clear training-data terms was found for shot logging. No newer model was looked for this time. The camera-move tagging half of #11 needs no model and could still be done on its own.
- **Phase 10 remainders:** the GPU compositor rendering scene-referred sequences, automatic trims measured in scene light, and VFX pull mattes in the working space (#1). Checking ST 2094-50 against FFmpeg 9's parser and an Android 17 or Chrome player, an in-between HDR rendition, curves edited by hand, and the metadata on reference outputs and HDR stills (#2). The RGB Mixer and Grain Match on the GPU, and the HDR Palette, Colour Warper and keyframes fitted from a LUT (#3, #4). Loop passes kept in step with the player's loop, lanes that ripple with the track, waveforms on lanes, and loop recording from the ADR panel (#5). The cue mix on a second device, metering per speaker and delays measured with a microphone (#6). Spotting lists with reel numbers, and PRS, ASCAP and BMI submission formats (#7). Karaoke and fades read from PGS, and DVD pictures from the export dialog (#8). Deep motion blur, transforms and defocus, Cryptomatte through the merge, and deep frames paged to disk (#9). Shadows from point lights, glTF's KHR extensions, motion blur and depth of field on models, and models on the GPU (#10). The clip's in and out, speed and place given to ARA plug-ins, a tempo map, shared audio sources and Audio Unit ARA (#11). Music detected apart from sounds, and the singing check tested on real songs (#12). Ambisonic fields and speech told from music within a clip (#13). The stacked timeline, node graph and curve editors described to screen readers, and VoiceOver and NVDA tried by hand (#14). Training on the GPU, and networks found again after a project moves (#15).
- **Phase 9 remainders** are as the Phase 10 roadmap listed them, except that a scene-linear working space and OpenFX colour management are done.
- **GPU compositor** (Phase 6 #1): still open are OCIO on the GPU, scene-referred sequences, depth, people and object masks, Refine Edges, the HDR Palette, the remaining effects, YUV frames uploaded as decoded, decoding into GPU memory, exports on the GPU, Vulkan and a speed check on a real GPU.
- **Blackmagic RAW** (Phase 6 #2) is still blocked on the SDK's terms.
- **Capture** (Phase 6 #13): DeckLink, AJA and NDI input remain blocked on closed SDKs. Deck control and batch capture are under Lower priority.
- **Camera motion data** (Phase 7 #6): Sony rtmd, DJI, Canon, Blackmagic RAW and Insta360, through telemetry-parser as planned. It needs sample files.
- **Phase 8 remainders still open:** Interop DCPs, KDMs for a cinema's FLM, and 4K 3D. The 2:3:3:2, 2:2:2:4 and shifted-field PAL cadences, and 25p to 50i. ADM audio in IMF, now part of #13 below, and several segments in IMF. Depth for reframed, 360°, stereo and nested views. IAMF beside the picture in one MP4. Other languages for shot descriptions. The other Avid flavours. 10-bit and HDR spatial video.
- **Phase 7 remainders:** lossy HTJ2K, true-stereo impulse responses, lip sync on more than the largest face, and expressions carried through AAF and OTIO.
- **Phase 6 remainders:** the dialogue-gated loudness range, now part of #7 below; roll-up, paint-on and service-2 708 captions; Tangent controls for Curves, the Colour Warper and the HDR Palette; split screen across several stills.
- **Prompted sound isolation and temporally consistent inpainting** still have no model with usable terms, as in Phases 9 and 10.
