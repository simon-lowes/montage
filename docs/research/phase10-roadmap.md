# Phase 10 roadmap (October 2026)

A ninth gap analysis, made as Phase 9 drew to a close. It compares Montage with Premiere 26.5 (to 26.5.2; Premiere for Android came out on 8 October) and After Effects 26.5 with its beta (an AI Assistant, Substance materials on 3D text and shapes), DaVinci Resolve 21.1 and 21.1.1 (2 October; no 21.1.2 or 21.2 has been announced), Final Cut Pro 12.4 (29 September; a video showing a Final Cut Pro 13 leaked on 1 October, but Apple has announced nothing) and Media Composer 2026.8. Adobe MAX is on 10–12 November in Miami Beach, after this analysis. It also looks at Baselight v7 and the Baselight 8 preview from IBC 2026 (due in 2027), Nuke 17.1, Pro Tools 2026.4.1 (no later release found), Nuendo 15, Descript (to its 12 October roundup), CapCut desktop 9.6, Kdenlive 26.08.2 and Shotcut 26.9.27 (Olive has had no release since 2024). Among open tools it looks at FFmpeg 9.0 "Lei" (4 August, to 9.0.2; the Phase 9 analysis looked only at 8.1), OpenColorIO 2.5.1 (no 2.6 yet), OpenTimelineIO 0.18.1 (nothing newer), OpenFX 1.5.1 (1.6 is in progress) and OpenEXR 3.5. It also covers SMPTE ST 2094-50, Celemony's ARA SDK, AP-BWE and the Libri2Mix separation models. Every candidate was checked against `README.md` and the `PROGRESS.md` checklists and their "Not done" and "Not yet" notes. These are already there and are not proposed again. From Phase 9: De-Clip, De-Plosive, De-Wind and Breath Control, floating-point pictures with OpenFX colour management, damaged-frame repair, dialogue intelligibility, sample-accurate sound, the phase, goniometer and spectrum meters, upmixing, Avid bins, film counts and lists, the sound effects library, particles, APV, and Open Media Transport. From Phase 8: the conform check, dialogue alignment, DCPs with KDMs, recovery of interrupted recordings, IMF versions, pulldown, steady depth and Follow Focus, automatic HDR trims, lens calibration, IAMF, HDR stills, film scans, words from the footage, the Avid OP-Atom hand-off and MV-HEVC. From earlier: Auto Colour, Auto Duck Music, Auto Mix, De-Esser, De-Click, De-Hum, De-Reverb, Spectral Repair, mic bleed removal, speaker detection, Separate Stems and the dialogue, music and effects split, track folders, the photosensitive flash check, Lens Flare, Motion Deblur, AI upscaling, Face Refinement, jump-cut morphs, review drawings and replies, OpenFX and audio plugin hosting, NDI and DeckLink reference output, Lottie and OGraf, and VR180. Recent releases also brought things Montage already has, so these are not proposed either: graphics templates filled from a spreadsheet (Premiere's data-driven templates); Auto Switch multicam by who is speaking (Media Composer 2026.8's multicam auto cut); the MCP server, the agent link and the Assistant panel (Resolve 21.1's MCP server and After Effects' AI Assistant); adjustment layers and automatic ducking (Shotcut 26.9); motion blur and Bézier keyframe easing (shown in the leaked Final Cut Pro 13); effect presets, HTJ2K in EXR and several caption streams in one master (Resolve 21.1). Dolby Vision, per-bin locking, direct publishing and generated media stay out.

Ranking is value to a professional editor, colourist or mixer × feasibility in C++/Qt/FFmpeg with optional local ONNX and GGUF models. Items that CI can test completely come first. Nothing below needs a paid SDK, an account, a cloud API or an API key. Every dependency and model is GPL-3.0-compatible unless **flagged**. Everything can be built and tested in the Linux cloud container (and on macOS and Windows in the Package workflow) except where a row says otherwise. Sizes: S ≤ 2, M 2–6, L 6–12 person-weeks.

## Earlier leftovers carried forward

- **Phase 9 #5 and #11 stay out.** For #5 (separating overlapping speakers), the cleanest model found is Asteroid's Conv-TasNet trained on Libri2Mix's clean set (`JorisCos/ConvTasNet_Libri2Mix_sepclean_16k`). Its training data is LibriSpeech alone (CC BY 4.0), so the data is clean. Its card, though, names both CC BY-SA 4.0 and CC BY-SA 3.0 for the weights, and only 4.0 is GPL-3.0-compatible (**flag**). Trained on clean read speech, it scores 15 dB SI-SDR on clean mixes and will do worse on location sound. SpeechBrain's SepFormer for Libri2Mix (Apache-2.0) works at 8 kHz and does not say which Libri2Mix set it was trained on. Neither is proposed. The author's word on the licence would settle the first. For #11 (shot logging), no vision-language model with clear training-data terms was found. The camera-move tagging half of #11 needs no model and could be done on its own.
- **Phase 9 remainders:** De-Plosive gated by the speech detector, and De-Wind on real wind (#1). A scene-linear working space and effects that clip marked in the Inspector, both now #1 below. Also half floats uploaded to the GPU directly, and OpenFX's OCIO and full styles (#2). Damaged-frame repair (#3): a Quality Check entry, runs of damaged frames, drop-out on one field, and a real damaged transfer. Intelligibility (#4): measured live during playback, a hearing-loss setting, surround measured per channel, and bands on the lane. Sample-accurate sound (#6): timecode fields in samples, dragging by samples at high zoom, keys between frames from the lanes, and interchange that keeps sample offsets. Audio analysis (#7): each surround pair, and mono compatibility per band. Upmix (#8): upmixing a bus or a clip, wides, and dynamics that hear the dialogue apart. Avid bins (#9): a bin from a current Media Composer, group clips as multicam clips, and bins written back. Film counts and lists (#10): FLEx logs, matchback and audio EDLs in feet and frames. The sound library (#12): a sound's markers and regions, renaming to UCS, and a shared library. Particles (#13): head trims carried, emitting from masks and shapes, sprites playing from birth, collisions, trails and the GPU. APV (#14): smart rendering, and APV in MXF. OMT (#15): alpha, OMT sources in the bin, and interop with libomtnet, vMix and OBS.
- **GPU compositor** (Phase 6 #1): still open are OCIO on the GPU, depth, people and object masks, Refine Edges, the HDR Palette, the remaining effects, YUV frames uploaded as decoded, decoding into GPU memory, exports on the GPU, Vulkan and a speed check on a real GPU. #1 below bakes OCIO's CPU processor into tables so that the GPU-matches-CPU rule holds.
- **Blackmagic RAW** (Phase 6 #2) is still blocked on the SDK's terms.
- **Capture** (Phase 6 #13): DeckLink, AJA and NDI input, deck control and batch capture remain.
- **Camera motion data** (Phase 7 #6): Sony rtmd, DJI, Canon, Blackmagic RAW and Insta360, through telemetry-parser as planned. It needs sample files.
- **Phase 8 remainders still open** (as the Phase 9 roadmap listed them): Interop DCPs, KDMs for a cinema's FLM, and 4K 3D. The 2:3:3:2, 2:2:2:4 and shifted-field PAL cadences, and 25p to 50i. IMSC 1.1 Image profile subtitles, now part of #8 below. ADM audio track files and several segments in IMF. Depth for reframed, 360°, stereo and nested views. IAMF beside the picture in one MP4. Other languages for shot descriptions. The other Avid flavours from #14. 10-bit and HDR spatial video.
- **Phase 7 remainders:** deep EXR (now #9), lossy HTJ2K, true-stereo impulse responses, lip sync on more than the largest face, and expressions carried through AAF and OTIO.
- **Phase 6 remainders:** the dialogue-gated loudness range, roll-up, paint-on and service-2 708 captions, Tangent controls for Curves, the Colour Warper and the HDR Palette, and split screen across several stills.
- **Phase 5 #14**'s "anything but flat titles" in a solved scene is #10 below.
- **Phase 2 #13**'s colour-managed scopes are part of #1 below.
- **Prompted sound isolation and temporally consistent inpainting** still have no model with usable terms: SAM Audio's licence, MiniMax-Remover and ProPainter, as in Phase 9.
