# Phase 15 roadmap (November 2026)

A fourteenth gap analysis, made on 9 November 2026 as Phase 14 was finished on draft PR #62 (head 197b49f, 62 commits, not yet merged; main is still 05f4b29 of 7 November), five days after the Phase 14 analysis of 4 November. **Three releases and one beta feature not in the Phase 14 analysis were found: DaVinci Resolve 21.2 (release notes dated 4 November), Logic Pro 13.0 (5 November, the first of Apple's Creator Studio updates), Kdenlive 26.08.3 (7 November, with KDE Gear 26.08.3 of 5 November; fixes only) and a Premiere beta feature, Generative Remove (posted 4 November; it runs in Adobe's cloud).** It compares Montage with Premiere 26.5.2 and After Effects 26.5 (no 26.6 or 27.0 was found; Adobe MAX runs from 10 to 12 November, starting the day after this analysis). Adobe's product updates page returned only its header to this analysis. Search results show the betas also read Sony's X-OCN better (date not shown), and bug reports name a Premiere beta 27.1.0 (Build 37). That build number comes from forum posts, not from Adobe. It covers Resolve 21.2 (Newsshooter, 4 November). It covers Final Cut Pro 12.4 (29 September): Apple's release notes page still has 12.4 at the top. MacRumors reported on 5 November that the Final Cut Pro, Motion and Compressor updates follow later in November. It covers Media Composer 2026.8 (Avid's version matrix, updated 29 October, has nothing newer) and Baselight v7 (no newer point release found; Baselight 8 has been shown but not shipped). It covers Nuke 17.1 (no 17.2 found), Pro Tools 2026.11 (3 November, as Phase 14 found; no 2026.11.1), Nuendo 15.0.40 (nothing newer found) and iZotope RX 12.1.0 (still the newest on iZotope's downloads page). For Shotcut it covers 26.9.27, with a v26.11 beta of 31 October whose notes list a fix for scratchy playback. Among open tools it covers FFmpeg 9.0.2: 9.1 is still in its release process, and no release was found. It also covers OpenColorIO 2.6.0, whose 2.6 release notes plan 2.6.1 for around the end of 2026, and OpenTimelineIO 0.18.1 and OpenFX 1.5.1. Searches found nothing newer for these, and their releases pages were not opened again. For AV2, dav2d's only release is still 0.0.1, and VideoLAN's repository refused this analysis again (a bot check). libvmaf's newest release is v3.2.1 (14 September), and the HDR VMAF v1 model of 26 October is not in a release yet. Five findings matter here:
- **DaVinci Resolve 21.2 (4 November).**
  - Blackmagic's Generation 6 colour science LUTs and film looks, False Color for Gen 5 and Gen 6, touch screens on the new MacBook Pro, and faster effects on M5 chips.
  - Editing touches: a menu action that restores the last active edit points, a compound clip made from an In–Out range in place, several timeline clips conformed to one media pool clip, and extracted audio that keeps its source timecode.
  - AI assistants that export frames as a grid, and about 30 fixes (AAF, OGraf, XAVC, DNG, HE-AAC and others).
- **Logic Pro 13.0 (5 November).** The Stem Splitter splits a drum stem into kick, snare, hi-hat, toms, cymbals and other percussion. A unified browser finds sounds by description and by likeness to a chosen sample, and an iPad can be used as a touch controller.
- **Two licences flagged since Phases 11 and 13 were read.** libvmaf (`LICENSE`) and Intel's SVT-JPEG-XS (`LICENSE.md`) are both BSD-2-Clause-Patent. That licence's own note says it is designed to be compatible with the GPL version 2 and to include an express patent grant. This opens VMAF on every build (#3) and JPEG XS with ST 2110-22 (#15).
- **SMPTE's 2026 IMF and ST 2110 publications.** None of these was in an earlier analysis. SMPTE's list of recently published documents (its page says it was updated on 22 July, yet it lists dates to 22 October) has:
  - ST 2067-21:2026-07, a revision of Application #2E (23 September).
  - ST 2067-206:2026-07, an IMF plug-in for event-based, text-based metadata (12 October). Per SMPTE's quarterly reports it is timed textual metadata in XML on a plug-in virtual track, with ST 2067-207 (video viewports) to follow.
  - ST 2110-41:2026-06, a revision of fast metadata (23 September).
  - ST 2094-2:2026-10, a revision (22 October).
  
  Montage writes Application #2E to the 2021 edition and carries no event metadata. Its -41 was written from public descriptions (Phase 14 #15).

  - Takes coloured across tracks (Pro Tools 2026.11): track groups and group comping, Phase 14 #3.

Dolby Vision, per-bin locking, direct publishing and generated media (Premiere's Generative Remove among them) stay out. Speaker separation and shot logging stay deferred.

Ranking is value to a professional editor, colourist, sound editor or finishing artist × feasibility in C++/Qt/FFmpeg with optional local ONNX and GGUF models. Items 1–12 can be tested completely in CI and come first; 13–15 each have one part that cannot be checked here, said in the row. Each row ends with what is out of reach. Nothing below needs a paid specification, an account, a cloud API or an API key. No new model is proposed, and no closed SDK is used. Every dependency is GPL-3.0-compatible unless **flagged**. The licences below were read from each project's repository on 9 November, unless a line says otherwise:
- libvmaf, for #3: BSD-2-Clause-Patent (`LICENSE`, "BSD+Patent"). It is two-clause BSD with an express, irrevocable, royalty-free patent grant and no termination clause. Its note says it was designed to be compatible with the GPL version 2. Nothing in it restricts what GPL-3.0 allows, so Phase 13's **flag** is lifted, for the owner to confirm. Its VMAF v1 SDR models ship in the same repository from v3.2.0 (20 June). zimg, which HDR VMAF needs, is WTFPL, as read for Phase 13.
- SVT-JPEG-XS, for #15: BSD-2-Clause-Patent (`LICENSE.md`, Intel, 2024), the same licence. Phase 11's **flag** is lifted likewise, for the owner to confirm.
- tinyusdz, built for #9's tests: Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE` on its `dev` branch).
- Weston, the Wayland compositor #13's tests run under: its `COPYING` on freedesktop.org's GitLab refused this analysis (a bot check), and a GitHub path returned 404, **flag** until read. It is a test tool only and is not linked or shipped.
- coturn, run by #6's tests: BSD-3-Clause, as read for Phase 14.
- dav2d: still unread, **flag**, as in Phase 14. #12 of Phase 14 remains built but not packaged.
- The ASIO SDK: as in Phase 14, **flag**. No build has it, as it cannot be fetched under its licence.
- VVenC (lower priority only): the Clear BSD licence (`LICENSE.txt`), which states that it grants no patent rights. It adds no restriction GPL-3.0 forbids.

Everything can be built and tested in the Linux cloud container (and on macOS and Windows in the Package workflow) except where a row says otherwise. Four items need a tool built in CI that is not built today: libvmaf v3.2.1 (#3), tinyusdz (#9), a Weston with colour management (#13) and SVT-JPEG-XS (#15). Sizes: S ≤ 2, M 2–6, L 6–12 person-weeks.

## Phase 14 remainders

What Phase 14's "Not done" notes leave, by item, and where each goes. Translations of the new texts are left for drafting in every item, as before.
- **#1, plug-ins in host processes:**
  - OpenFX on-screen controls still run in Montage's own process. Now #2.
  - A crash while loading in a shared host blames nothing, and instances of one plug-in share a host. Now #2.
  - The macOS and Windows paths were not run, and Audio Units were not tried in a host. Now #2.
  - OpenGL renders are read back rather than shared between processes. Now #14.
  - A background analysis that meets a bypassed plug-in counts as an export failure. Now #12.
  - MIDI and instrument plug-ins are not hosted: Montage has no instrument tracks, so this stays out.
- **#2, effects on the GPU:**
  - Still on the CPU: Lens Blur (Depth), people masks, depth from the still model, Refine Edges, the Keyer's clean plate made from the picture, Mesh Warp over alpha and with motion blur, Displace by Layer, Painterly, shape layers, Light Rays with Shadows Block Rays, Aperture Glare, splats, the composite graph's nodes, Smooth Cut, and an adjustment layer inside a transition. There is no radial blur effect, and the OCIO effect has no live exposure, contrast or gamma. Now #1.

  - Speed on real GPUs stays out of reach. The PQ Crosstalk rounding stays, as it follows from the input.
- **#3, track groups:**
  - Punch-ins, MCP `record` and Record Voiceover go to one track. Now #10.
  - AAF and FCPXML have no field for a track's colour, so that stays.
  - Groups of folders, and switches that differ by track, are lower priority.
  - The near-+12 dB limit on grouped moves stays, by design.
- **#4, buffer models:**
  - Only the first schedule is checked, and sub-picture checks and low-delay pictures are simplified. Now #12.
  - AV1's decoder model is not checked, VP9 is not copied, MPEG-2 at a variable rate is joined without a model, and the Export dialog cannot state an HRD. Now #12.

- **#5, browser review:**
  - Bandwidth is estimated from loss only. TURN's time-limited credentials are not made, relay-only can still send from this computer's own address, and coturn was not run. Now #6.

  - Safari and HEVC Main 10 in browsers stay out of reach on Linux.
- **#6, live packages and broadcast sound:**

  - DRM stays out.
- **#7, IMF for broadcasters:**

- **#9, speed and jobs:**
  - Analyses and transcodes started from the app's dialogs do not go through the job store. Now #12.
  - An MCP call resumed from the start, the old DFT code kept beside the new, and speed on users' machines stay as they are.
- **#10, motion graphics:**
  - A title follows only its own clip's masks, and feather and expansion do not change the path. Now #8.

  - A 3D title's animators set depth only. Now #8.
