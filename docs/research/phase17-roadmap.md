- **Motion blur that follows the picture.** Final Cut Pro 13 blurs retimed footage by its retimed motion. A clip's keyed Transform has no motion blur at all: of Montage's effects, only Mesh Warp, Grid Warp, Motion Blur and the titles' letters take a shutter (`core/Effects.cpp`). This is #4.
